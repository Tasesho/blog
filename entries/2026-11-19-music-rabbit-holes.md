# Doujin Music

According to its definition, **doujin music** refers to a subcategory of doujin activities. The term _doujin_ is associated with independently produced and self-published works, which can be either completely original creations or derivative works based on existing productions.

However, I think the modern doujin scene is particularly interesting because of its relationship with fan communities. People started creating and sharing content inspired by their favorite series, games or novels. Over time, these communities became creative ecosystems of their own, where musicians and artists could experiment with existing material or create something entirely new.

The most famous example of this is undoubtedly **Touhou Project**. ZUN has done irreversible damage to the doujin culture (in the best possible way). His work has inspired an enormous number of artists, musicians, and independent creators. At this point, it's almost as if _doujin_ has become synonymous with Touhou for some people.

## Touhou

**Touhou Project** is a video game franchise created by ZUN, an independent Japanese developer. It started as a series of bullet hell games, but its influence goes far beyond gameplay and character design. One of its most influential aspects is undoubtedly its music.

It's so influential that many of the bands I'm going to mention later were either born from or heavily inspired by Touhou's musical and cultural impact. Its compositions have been rearranged into practically every genre you can imagine, from jazz and electronic music to hardcore and extreme metal.

### Rhythm Games and Touhou

Another important part of this whole ecosystem is rhythm games. Titles like **osu!**, **beatmania IIDX**, and **Dance Dance Revolution** have helped introduce players to music from different genres and independent artists.

Touhou music and its arrangements have also found a home in rhythm games, creating another way for people to discover these compositions and the artists behind them.

For me, rhythm games have been one of the biggest gateways into discovering underground music. Sometimes, all it takes is a random beatmap to introduce you to an entirely new band.

## Foreground Eclipse

**Foreground Eclipse** was a Japanese doujin music circle active between 2009 and 2013. According to Touhou Wiki, their music incorporates genres such as Vocal, Screamo, and Post-Hardcore.

Their members were:

- Merami (Vocals)
    
- Teto (Drums & Screams)
    
- Suzouri (Bass)
    

They became known for their distinctive arrangements of Touhou soundtracks, combining aggressive vocals, melodic passages, and remarkably high production values.

## Imperial Circus Dead Decadence

**Imperial Circus Dead Decadence (ICDD)** is a Japanese doujin melodic death metal band that has been active since 2007. With nearly two decades of history, they have built a distinctive sound within the Japanese extreme metal scene.

One of their most recognizable features is their **insane guttural vocals**, accompanied by heavy drums and elaborate instrumental arrangements.

Their music blends several metal subgenres, including Death Metal, Symphonic Metal, and Power Metal, among others. The result is an incredibly theatrical and aggressive sound that is hard to mistake for anything else.

## Draw the Emotional

**Draw the Emotional** is a Japanese doujin music circle founded in September 2009 by Vocaloid producer **Yuyoyuppe** (music) and illustrator **Shoujiki**.

Known for blending Melodic Metalcore, Post-Hardcore, and Electronicore, the circle gained prominence through its arrangements and original compositions for both the _Touhou Project_ and _Vocaloid_ communities.

Their music is particularly interesting because of the way it combines electronic elements with heavy guitar riffs and emotional vocal performances.

# You've Listened to Touhou!

Yes, you probably have! Throughout the years, Touhou music has made some remarkable contributions to internet culture. Some of its arrangements have become so popular that you might recognize them without even knowing where they originally came from.

Let's take a look at a couple of examples.

- **Bad Apple!!**: An absolute classic of the Touhou community and internet culture. Its iconic music video features a monochromatic silhouette animation depicting several Touhou characters. Over time, the video itself became a template for countless recreations across an insane number of platforms and media formats. It's practically part of the internet's DNA at this point.
    
- **タイニーリトル・アジアンタム (Tiny Little Adiantum)**: You might remember this one from around 2018–2020. The meme (Memes The DNA of soul...) usually featured a cute anime girl dancing along to the song, which was already catchy and adorable enough on its own. It became one of those internet trends that you could recognize almost immediately after hearing the first few seconds.
    
- **The osu! Community**: Over the years, osu! has played an important role in introducing Japanese doujin music and its subgenres to Western audiences. Since its early days, the osu! community has embraced Touhou music, with countless arrangements and original compositions finding their way into the game's beatmap library. For many players, rhythm games like osu! have been an important gateway into discovering the Touhou Project and its musical scene.
