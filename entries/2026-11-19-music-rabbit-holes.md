# Doujin Music

According to its definition, **doujin music** refers to a subcategory of doujin activities. The term _doujin_ is associated with independently produced and self-published works, which can be either completely original creations or derivative works based on existing productions.

However, I think the modern doujin scene is particularly interesting because of its relationship with fan communities. People started creating and sharing content inspired by their favorite series, games or novels. Over time, these communities became creative ecosystems of their own, where musicians and artists could experiment with existing material or create something entirely new.

The most famous example of this is undoubtedly **Touhou Project**. ZUN has done irreversible damage to the doujin culture (in the best possible way). His work has inspired an enormous number of artists, musicians, and independent creators. At this point, it's almost as if _doujin_ has become synonymous with Touhou for some people.

# Touhou

**Touhou Project** is a video game franchise created by ZUN, an independent Japanese developer. It started as a series of bullet hell games, but its influence goes far beyond gameplay and character design. One of its most influential aspects is undoubtedly its music.

It's so influential that many of the bands I'm going to mention later were either born from or heavily inspired by Touhou's musical and cultural impact. Its compositions have been rearranged into practically every genre you can imagine, from jazz and electronic music to hardcore and extreme metal.

[https://media.tenor.com/ZFa7z5J7hdIAAAAi/reisen-touhou.gif](https://media.tenor.com/ZFa7z5J7hdIAAAAi/reisen-touhou.gif)

### ZUNpets

If you've ever listened to Touhou music, you've probably noticed that distinctive brass-like synthesizer sound. This characteristic sound, commonly referred to by fans as **ZUNpets**, has become one of the most recognizable elements of ZUN's compositions.

It's one of those things that makes his music instantly recognizable, even when you don't know which game or character the song comes from.

### Rhythm Games and Touhou

Another important part of this whole ecosystem is rhythm games. Titles like **osu!**, **beatmania IIDX**, and **Dance Dance Revolution** have helped introduce players to music from different genres and independent artists.

Touhou music and its arrangements have also found a home in rhythm games, creating another way for people to discover these compositions and the artists behind them.

For me, rhythm games have been one of the biggest gateways into discovering underground music. Sometimes, all it takes is a random beatmap to introduce you to an entirely new band.
